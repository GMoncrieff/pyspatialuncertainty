# Round 6 scorecard

Universal error = mean |coverage − 0.90| over every cell (landscape side × map × design × n × class × block size), equal weights, 90 % intervals. `err`: universal error; `worst`: lowest cell coverage; `within`: share of cells with coverage in 0.85–0.95; `acc`: median over cells of median |error| relative to `ok`; `out_land` / `wild_land`: landscape estimates outside [0, 1] / with |error| > 4 SD; `bside:k`, `design:k`, `n:k`: universal error within that level. Rules: `normal`, `logit`, `hybrid` (union of the clipped normal and the logit interval), `arcsine`. Advance rule: lower error than the base, no marginal (block size, n, design, landscape, map, class) worse by more than 0.02, accuracy within 10 %, no more out-of-range or wild estimates; differences paired over realizations with bootstrap 90 % CIs (`d_lo`, `d_hi`). Out-of-range counts are not used for continuous maps.

Arm names are `fit+modifiers`: fits `cur` (zero-mean ML, the paper), `ok` (REML, free family), `es` (Exp/Sph), `exp` (Exp), `thr` (threshold-shaped REML); modifiers `x2` (X = [1, σ]) / `x3` (X = [1, 1/σ, m/σ]), `vp` / `vg` (Laplace / grid-posterior sigma points), `disp` (Brier-ratio dispersion), `scale` (REML sill), `ci` / `cg` / `cs` / `cc` (calibrator draws: iid bootstrap / pair-correlation sandwich / model sandwich / cluster bootstrap).
