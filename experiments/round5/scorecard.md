# Round 5 scorecard

Coverage error = mean |coverage − 0.90| over classes × sample sizes × designs. `land_err` at the whole landscape, `t256_err` at 256×256 tiles; `cov4`/`cov64` mean coverage of 4×4 and 64×64 tiles; `worst_land` the lowest landscape coverage of any setting; `acc` median |error| relative to `ok`; `wild`/`out` landscape estimates with |error ÷ SD| > 4 / outside [0, 1]. C = B with logit intervals from 256×256: its errors are B's `land_err_lgt` and `t256_err_lgt` (in the JSON summaries). For continuous maps `out` is not meaningful.
